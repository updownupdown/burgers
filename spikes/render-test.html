  • Low-poly is flat-shaded, close to an Aloft-style look.
  • Toon adds three-step shading and ink outlines.
  • Pixel renders the toon scene at low resolution and scales it up with hard pixels, for a pixel-art feel with no hand-drawn sprites.
  • No image files are used. Buns, patty, cheese drip, lettuce ruffles and seeds are all shapes built in code, so the game can animate and recolour them freely.
